StrongFace Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Summary: StrongFace does not require an account, does not include advertising or tracking, and does not send your practice data to the developer.

Data stored on your device

The app stores setup preferences, settings, and practice history locally on your iPhone so its core features work. This data is not transmitted to the developer. Apple may include app data in a device backup according to your iCloud and device settings.

Data collection and tracking

The current StrongFace app does not collect personal data, does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Permissions

StrongFace does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or health records for its documented features.

Children

The app is a general-audience fitness and wellness tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If app features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before those practices take effect.
