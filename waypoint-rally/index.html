Waypoint Rally · iPhone

Race today's island loop.

Steer through ten checkpoints, choose your line, and try to beat your best time.

How to play

The car accelerates automatically. Drag the steering pad to follow the Palm Coast Loop and hold BRAKE when a turn needs it. The daily route and your attempts are stored on your device.

When the online service is configured and reachable, you can compare a daily result and see replay ghosts. If the service is unavailable, the game keeps your local run and reports that it was not synced.

Questions and support

The leaderboard did not load

Check your connection and try again later. Your local attempt remains available. Online comparisons require a configured build and a reachable service.

How do I request deletion of online data?

Waypoint Rally does not currently offer an in-game server-data deletion control. Contact support with the app name, your display name, and the approximate UTC day you played. Do not include passwords, session tokens, or other account credentials.
