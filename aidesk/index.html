Your AI chat workspace.

A fast, native Mac app for AI chat — with voice input, deep web research, image and document understanding, and memory that carries across chats. Subscribe and start chatting; nothing else to set up.

About

Deskcrew is a native macOS workspace for AI chat. A Deskcrew Pro subscription includes metered AI usage through Deskcrew's own secure service, so you can start working right away.

FEATURES
• Multi-chat workspace with streaming responses and full markdown
• A crew of assistant presets — a general Assistant, plus Research, Marketing, and Finance agents
• Voice input with live transcription (⌥Space)
• Deep web research that reads sources and returns a cited report
• Ask about images and documents — attach a PDF, code file, or image
• Workspace memory that carries context across chats
• Menu-bar quick assistant, even when the window is closed

No account and no tracking. Your chats are stored locally on your Mac.

Getting started

  1. Start the 7-day free trial from the welcome screen.
  2. Open a chat — responses stream in live. Pick a Fast or Standard model per chat.
  3. Drop in files to discuss them; press ⌥Space for voice input.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to set anything up?

No. Deskcrew Pro includes metered AI usage through Deskcrew's secure service — subscribe and start chatting. There are no keys to configure.

Is my data private?

Yes. Deskcrew has no account and no analytics. Your chats are stored locally on your Mac.

How does billing work?

Deskcrew Pro is a subscription with a 7-day free trial and a fair-use monthly allowance. Payment is charged to your Apple Account and renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in System Settings.
