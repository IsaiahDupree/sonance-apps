About MatterForge

MatterForge is a material-evolution discovery game. Start with eight basic materials, choose two samples, and forge them together to discover new substances. Successful combinations expand an archive spanning four scientific eras. Each discovery includes a plain-language summary, a science note, and the process behind it.

Getting started

  1. Open Forge and tap or drag two available samples into the forge.
  2. Start the reaction. A successful combination adds its result to your available materials and Archive.
  3. Use a hint when you need a lead. Research points and progress are shown in the app.
  4. Open Archive to review discoveries by era, or Guide for instructions and research statistics.

Frequently asked questions

Does MatterForge need an account or internet connection?

No. MatterForge does not require an account, and your game progress stays on your iPhone.

Why did two materials not produce a discovery?

Only specific pairs produce a new material, and the order of the pair does not matter. Try another pairing or use a hint. Your attempt still contributes to the statistics shown in Guide.

How do I start over?

Open Guide, choose Reset research progress, and confirm. This permanently clears your discoveries and statistics on that device, then restores the eight starter materials.

What accessibility features are supported?

MatterForge supports VoiceOver labels and spoken context for its main controls and discoveries. Haptic feedback accompanies important interactions where supported.

What information does the app collect?

MatterForge does not collect personal data, run analytics, show ads, or track you. Read the app-specific Privacy Policy for details.
