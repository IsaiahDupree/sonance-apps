FolioFoundry Support

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

FolioFoundry stores book projects, manuscript text, metadata, and optional copied cover art on your Mac. It creates EPUB, PDF, and web export files at the location you choose. The app does not upload manuscripts or cover art, operate an account service, or send book content to a server.

Files and permissions

You choose files to import and export through macOS file dialogs. Imported manuscripts and project packages remain in local storage managed by the app or in locations you choose. You can remove project files using Finder. Exported copies are not removed when a project is deleted.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The inspected source contains no analytics or advertising SDK and declares no collected data in its privacy manifest. The app does not use tracking. This policy describes FolioFoundry's current source behavior; App Store privacy disclosures are separately checked for each release binary and its dependencies.

Children

FolioFoundry is a general-purpose book-authoring utility and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior changes. Questions may be sent to isaiahdupree33@gmail.com.
