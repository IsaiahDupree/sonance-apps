FolioFoundry · Mac

Support

FolioFoundry is a macOS book-production workspace for organizing manuscripts and exporting EPUB, PDF, and web editions.

Getting started

  1. Create a book or import a Markdown, plain-text, RTF, RTFD, or DOCX manuscript.
  2. Organize front matter, chapters, and back matter; add book metadata and choose a theme.
  3. Review the preview and editorial checks, then export the format you need.

Files and privacy

Book projects and exports are stored locally on your Mac. The app does not send manuscript content to a server. See the FolioFoundry privacy policy.

Contact
