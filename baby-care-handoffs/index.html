Care handoffs

A clear record of the care you entered.

Development preview. Simulator acceptance and store preparation are in progress. This Watch release is not currently offered for download here.

Getting started

  1. Add a child, then record feeding, diaper or sleep events.
  2. Review recorded times and notes. End an open sleep entry or correct an event in history.
  3. Export the selected child’s records or erase that child and request paired erasure.

What to expect

Care handoffs is a caregiver-entered journal. It does not monitor a child, offer medical advice or provide household cloud sharing.

Pairing and offline use

Open the app on both paired devices when checking a transfer. Waiting to send is different from a confirmed result. A disconnected device may continue to show older saved records.
