Aerospace Review · iOS

Support

Review the exact drafts and evidence in your private workspace from an iPhone. Decisions require device-owner authentication and are sent to your configured workspace.

Set up a private connection

Configure the private HTTPS origin and dedicated reviewer credential supplied by your workspace administrator. The app verifies the reviewer identity before saving the connection. The workspace must be reachable from the phone’s network.

Review a draft

Open the queue item, inspect its recipient, text, and evidence, then choose an available action. Approve, reject, edit, or request cancellation for the exact current draft. The app refreshes state after a decision; approval is not proof that a message was sent.

Connection or authentication issue

Confirm the private HTTPS endpoint is reachable and that the reviewer credential is current. Reconnect after rotating the credential. The app refuses redirects and does not provide a public tunnel or credential bypass.
