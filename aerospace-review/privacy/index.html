Aerospace Review Support

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Aerospace Review connects to a private workspace endpoint that you configure. It reads draft actions, recipient and relationship information, contract/staffing evidence, and delivery receipts from that workspace. When you choose an action, the app sends your decision or edited text to that endpoint. This information may identify people and should be treated as sensitive.

Where data goes

Requests are sent over HTTPS to the private workspace origin that you configure. The app does not upload data to an Aerospace Review developer-operated service. Your workspace operator controls the server, its data sources, access, retention, and logs; consult that operator for server-side handling. The app blocks HTTP except for loopback simulator testing and refuses redirects.

Credentials and device storage

The dedicated reviewer credential is stored in the iOS Keychain and can be removed by disconnecting. The selected private endpoint is stored in app preferences. Network responses use an ephemeral URL session with no response cache.

Authentication, analytics, and tracking

Approving, editing, or cancelling a draft requires device-owner authentication on a physical iPhone. The inspected source includes no advertising or analytics SDK and does not track users across apps or websites. This policy describes the app client; the private workspace may independently collect or retain information.

Children

Aerospace Review is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior changes. Questions may be sent to isaiahdupree33@gmail.com.
