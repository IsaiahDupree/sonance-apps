Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026. Developer: Isaiah Dupree.

This policy describes ReelVela's current development app. Public customer service access is still being prepared. Connecting a service or loading a remote thumbnail sends network requests; on-device storage does not mean all app activity stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores bookmarked post evidence, source snapshots and service settings in its local app storage. These collections are not currently synchronized between devices. A paired Script Hub access token is stored in Apple's Keychain and bound to the configured service origin.

Research and connected services

When you load or refresh research, ReelVela sends the selected platform and request parameters to the configured research service. Filtering the loaded post sample with search text happens on your device. The research server receives connection information needed to handle requests, such as an IP address and user-agent information. Server operators may retain request diagnostics under their service's policies.

Post cards can request thumbnail images directly from the image URLs returned by the research service. Those image hosts receive connection information such as your IP address and the requested image URL, and their privacy practices apply. Thumbnails may need a network connection even when the saved post metadata is available locally.

When you pair a device, the pairing code is sent to the configured Script Hub service. If you request a draft, your topic, selected platform, duration and access token are sent to that service. Script Hub processes draft requests on the server and may send the supplied topic to its configured AI providers. Do not submit confidential information unless you trust the connected service and its data handling.

Provider credentials are not distributed in the app. Local bookmarks are not automatically uploaded as a collection.

Selected photos and videos

The Analyzer reads only the file you select. Apple's Vision and AVFoundation frameworks inspect dimensions, duration, faces and visible text locally. The Analyzer does not upload the selected image or video and does not identify people from detected face rectangles.

Tracking and purchases

The current native app has no advertising, developer analytics SDK or cross-app tracking. There are no enabled ReelVela in-app purchase products in this release configuration.

External links

Opening an original Instagram source, Apple's account tools or another external link leaves ReelVela. The destination's privacy practices apply. The app does not download or republish source videos.

Deletion and access

You can remove bookmarks using their in-app removal controls. In Settings, Forget this device removes the local pairing token; this is not a server-side token revocation or a deletion request. Uninstalling removes local app data according to the operating system's behavior and does not guarantee removal of Keychain entries or server records. For deletion of records held by a connected service, contact its operator. Contact the developer below for help with developer-operated services.

Support and changes

If you contact support, your message and any details you choose to include are used to respond to you. Please do not send passwords or API tokens. Material changes to app or hosted-service data handling will be reflected in this policy.
