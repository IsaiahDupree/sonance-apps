The short version

Podcast Studio is a cloud-connected service. It uses your email address to provide an account, stores the recordings and project content you choose to upload, sends media to OpenAI for transcription, and sends transcript text to OpenAI to generate episode and social content. Supabase also retains authentication and service-log metadata, including the request IP address, for account security and service operation. Podcast Studio does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs and does not use your information for cross-app tracking.

How processing works

  • The Mac app sends the media you select to storage associated with your authenticated Podcast Studio workspace.
  • The processing service retrieves the media and sends the recording, or its audio, to OpenAI’s transcription API to produce text and timestamped segments.
  • Transcript text is sent to OpenAI’s text-generation API to create the episode metadata and social copy described above.
  • The transcript, generated content, and your segment edits are stored with the episode so they remain available in the app.

Service providers

The following processors receive information only as needed to operate Podcast Studio:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database storage, media storage, and service logging. Supabase authentication logs automatically associate an account event with request metadata such as IP address and user agent; log retention depends on the service plan. See Supabase’s Privacy Policy and Auth Audit Logs documentation.
  • Vercel hosts the backend that coordinates uploads and processing. See Vercel’s Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI processes media for transcription and transcript text for content generation. See OpenAI’s Privacy Policy.

These providers may process information in locations described in their own policies. Podcast Studio does not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

Account and episode information is kept so your workspace remains available across sessions. Uploaded media, transcripts, segment choices, and generated content remain associated with your account until they are deleted or you request deletion, except where limited retention is reasonably necessary for security, backup recovery, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Supabase authentication and service logs are retained for a plan-dependent period and may remain for security, abuse prevention, and service reliability after workspace content is deleted.

To request account and content deletion: email isaiahdupree33@gmail.com from the email address used to sign in. Use the subject Podcast Studio data deletion and state whether you want a specific episode deleted or your account and all associated media, transcripts, and generated content deleted. You may be asked to verify account ownership. Do not attach the media to your request.

After a verified request, data under our control will be deleted or de-identified as applicable, subject to the limited exceptions above. Residual copies may remain temporarily in processor backups and are governed by the processors’ retention and deletion practices. Signing out of the app ends the local session but does not by itself delete cloud data.

Security

Podcast Studio uses authenticated accounts and encrypted HTTPS connections when communicating with its cloud services. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please use an email account you control and do not share one-time sign-in codes.

Children

Podcast Studio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for or collect personal information from children under 13.
