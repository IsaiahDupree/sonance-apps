SunTrace Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

SunTrace uses the information you provide to calculate personal sunlight estimates, save your sessions, and show local UV conditions. We do not sell personal information, serve ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Information you provide

  • Account information, including your email address and an account identifier.
  • Profile inputs used for estimates, including age, skin type, goals, and notification preferences.
  • Sunlight sessions, including timestamps, duration, estimated UV exposure, progress, optional notes, and any location saved with a session.

Location and forecast data

With your permission, SunTrace reads your foreground location while the app is in use. Coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve local UV and cloud forecasts and may be stored with a session you save. SunTrace does not request continuous background location access.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate your account, calculate and display sunlight estimates, save your history and progress, provide local forecasts, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and maintain the service.

Service providers

  • Supabase provides account authentication and hosted database storage.
  • Open-Meteo provides UV and weather forecast data from coordinates supplied for a forecast request.
  • Apple may process information when you download or update the app.

Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking services in SunTrace.

Storage and security

Private SunTrace records are stored in Supabase and protected by account-based access controls. Network traffic uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No system can promise absolute security, but access is limited to what is needed to operate and support the app.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and SunTrace data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account from Profile → Delete Account. Deletion removes your authentication account and private SunTrace records. You may also contact us for help with a deletion request.

Children

SunTrace is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Health disclaimer

SunTrace provides informational estimates and habit-tracking tools. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Changes

We may update this policy as SunTrace changes. The effective date above will identify the latest version.
