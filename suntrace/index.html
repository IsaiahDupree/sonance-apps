What SunTrace does

Exposure sessions

Start and stop an outdoor session to record elapsed time, local UV conditions, and your estimated sunlight dose.

Forecast planning

See current and upcoming UV conditions based on your approximate location so you can plan time outside.

History and progress

Review prior sessions, weekly totals, streaks, and badges in one place.

Common questions

Why does SunTrace request location?

Your location is used to look up local UV conditions and nearby sun spots. SunTrace requests access only while you use the app.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile, scroll to Account & Support, then choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and private SunTrace data.

My UV reading looks wrong.

Pull to refresh and confirm location access is enabled. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo and can differ from a nearby physical sensor.

How do I contact support?

Important health note

SunTrace provides estimates for awareness and habit tracking. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prevent, or treat any condition. UV exposure can damage skin; follow local health guidance and advice from a qualified clinician.
