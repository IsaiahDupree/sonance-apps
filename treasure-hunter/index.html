Treasure Hunter: Daily Island

Find today's ten hidden relics.

Move across a bright island, pulse your detector, and follow its clues to the artifacts.

How to play

Use the on-screen move pad to explore in any direction. Tap SCAN to check nearby caches; the detector reports the nearest unfound relic's strength, distance, and bearing. Find all ten artifacts to finish the daily hunt. Everyone gets the same daily layout, so compare your best time with today's leaderboard when online play is available.

Each attempt is saved on your device, and you can retry the same daily island. Online leaderboards and replay ghosts are optional network features; if the service is unavailable, the hunt remains playable offline and the app labels the result as local.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game need an account?

No sign-up is required. Online scores use an anonymous service identity and a generated hunter name. You can play offline without connecting to the leaderboard.

Does the game use my location?

No. The island and relics are generated in the game; Treasure Hunter does not need your real-world location.
