Treasure Hunter: Daily Island

Find today's ten hidden relics.

Move across a bright island, pulse your detector, and follow its clues to the artifacts.

How to play

Use the on-screen move pad to explore in any direction. Tap SCAN to check nearby caches; the detector reports the nearest unfound relic's strength, distance, and bearing. Find all ten artifacts to finish the daily hunt.

The island layout changes with the date and is generated on your device. Retry the daily hunt, save your attempts, and try to beat your personal best. Your scores and replays stay on your device. Treasure Hunter has no online leaderboard or player-submitted replays.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game need an account or internet connection?

No. Treasure Hunter does not require an account or internet connection. Your daily hunt, attempt history, personal-best replay, and hunt-film export work offline.

Does the game use my location?

No. The island and relics are generated in the game. Treasure Hunter does not need your real-world location.

Where are my attempts and replays stored?

They are stored on your device and are not uploaded to a game server. If you choose to export or share a hunt film, the system share sheet lets you select the destination.
