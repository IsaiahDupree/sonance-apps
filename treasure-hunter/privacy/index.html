Treasure Hunter: Daily Island

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Treasure Hunter is a local-first game. It does not require an account or internet connection, and the game does not send gameplay data or identifiers to the publisher or a game server. The checked-in Unity project has Unity Services and Unity Analytics disabled and includes no third-party analytics package.

Information stored on your device

The app stores a random installation identifier, a generated hunter display name, attempts, personal-best replays, a cosmetic choice, and replay frames locally. Replay frames contain in-game timing, position and orientation, speed, vehicle state, collected-artifact markers, and camera events. They describe gameplay on a generated island and do not contain your real-world location.

Network access

The Treasure Hunter app shell does not initialize the shared leaderboard or replay service. It does not create an online game identity, submit scores or display names, or download other players' replays. The daily island and local records work without a network connection.

Sharing and video export

If you choose to export or share a replay or daily hunt film, the app creates the video on your device and opens the system save or share interface. The destination you select receives the content you choose to save or share under that destination's own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Attempts, replay files, the generated installation identifier, and cosmetic settings remain on your device until you clear the app's data or remove the app. There is no online profile or server copy to delete.

Location and children

The game does not request real-world location access. Treasure Hunter is a general-audience game. Parents or guardians with privacy questions can contact isaiahdupree33@gmail.com.
