Treasure Hunter: Daily Island

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Treasure Hunter stores attempts and replay history on your device. The game does not require sign-up, display ads, or include a third-party analytics SDK in the checked-in Unity project. Online leaderboard and ghost features send pseudonymous game data to the service described below. You can play offline.

Information processed on your device

The app saves a random installation identifier, a generated hunter display name, your attempts, personal bests, cosmetic choice, and replay frames locally. Replay frames include elapsed time, in-game position and orientation, speed, vehicle state, collected-artifact markers, and camera events. They describe gameplay within the generated island, not your real-world location.

When you use online features

If backend access is configured and available, the game creates an anonymous Supabase authentication session without asking for your email or password. When you submit a run, the service receives the app and daily-challenge identifiers, your anonymous service user ID, install UUID, hunter display name, time, score, number of artifacts found, completion state, and replay data. Supabase stores this information for the leaderboard and replay-ghost features. Your generated display name and best run may be shown to other players in the daily leaderboard or as a replay ghost.

The Unity client uses Supabase's public anonymous key and HTTPS endpoints. It does not include the database service-role key. Supabase provides hosting, authentication, and database services; see Supabase's privacy notice. Standard network and service logs may also be processed by the provider.

If the backend is unavailable or not configured, the game keeps the attempt locally and reports that online results are unavailable. It does not fabricate leaderboard entries.

Sharing and video export

If you choose to export or share a replay or Daily Film, the game creates the video on your device and presents the system save/share interface. The destination service receives the content only if you choose to save or share it there.

Retention and deletion

Local attempts and replay files remain until you clear the app's data or remove the app. Online profiles, submitted runs, and replay data are stored in the service database. The current client does not include an account or server-data deletion control, and the checked-in database migrations do not define an automatic deletion schedule. To ask about a data deletion or correction request, contact isaiahdupree33@gmail.com with “Treasure Hunter privacy request.”

Children and contact

Treasure Hunter is a general-audience game. Parents or guardians with privacy questions can contact isaiahdupree33@gmail.com.

Policy updates

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
