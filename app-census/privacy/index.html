App Census Support

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

App Census inspects applications installed in the system and user Applications folders on your Mac. This policy describes information handled by the macOS app.

Information kept on your Mac

App Census reads local application names, bundle identifiers, versions, file locations, modification dates, allocated sizes, code-signature validity, and whether an App Store receipt is present. The system Applications folder is scanned by default. If you choose another Applications folder, macOS grants access to that folder; App Census stores a security-scoped bookmark on your Mac so you do not need to select it again each time. You can remove that access from the Folders menu. The inventory, bookmark, search/filter settings, preferences, and local feature-use counts are stored or processed on your Mac. App Census does not send this inventory or bookmark to the developer or a server.

Optional diagnostics export

If you choose to export diagnostics, the file contains aggregate application counts, counts of signed and receipt-bearing apps, selected filters, a search-active flag, language/theme preferences, and locally recorded feature-use counts. It does not include app names, bundle identifiers, file paths, or the folder-access bookmark. macOS presents a save dialog and you choose where the file is saved. If you later share it with support, you choose to send it.

Data collection and tracking

App Census has no account, advertising, analytics service, or tracking service. The app does not collect information from the device for transmission to the developer or third parties. Apple may process information under its own services and policies when you download the app or use macOS features.
