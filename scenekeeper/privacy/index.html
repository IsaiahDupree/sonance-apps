SceneKeeper Support

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

SceneKeeper manages groups of apps on your Mac. This policy describes information handled by the macOS app.

Information stored on your Mac

Scene definitions include the name you enter and references to apps you select, such as their names, bundle identifiers, and local paths. SceneKeeper stores these definitions in local JSON files. An interrupted new-scene draft, language and appearance preferences, search/filter/sort choices, and local feature-use counters are stored in this app's UserDefaults domain.

When you apply a scene, SceneKeeper reads the running apps on your Mac and uses macOS APIs to launch selected apps, hide apps, or request that apps quit according to your choice. SceneKeeper does not send scene names, selected app paths, running-app state, or usage metrics over the network.

Optional diagnostics export

If you choose Diagnostics and save a file, it contains a timestamp, scene and app-reference counts, counts by outside-app action, active filter/sort state, whether a search is active, the number of apps in an interrupted draft, and local feature-use counts with language and appearance settings. It does not contain scene names, app names, bundle identifiers, or app paths. You choose where to save the file and whether to share it with support.

Data collection and tracking

SceneKeeper has no account, advertising, network service, third-party analytics SDK, or tracking service. The app does not transmit information to the developer. Apple may process information under its own services and policies when you download the app or use macOS features.
