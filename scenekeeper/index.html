Save a group of Mac apps as a scene, then choose whether apps outside it stay open, hide, or quit after confirmation.

Create and apply a scene

  • Create a named scene and choose installed apps with the macOS file picker.
  • Select Leave, Hide, or Quit for apps outside the scene.
  • Choose Apply Scene to launch selected apps that are not running and apply the outside-app choice.
  • Every Quit action requires a fresh confirmation because unsaved work in other apps could be lost.

SceneKeeper protects Finder, Dock, SystemUIServer, and itself from hide and quit actions. It saves scene definitions and interrupted new-scene drafts locally. It does not restore window positions.

Common questions

An app is missing from a scene. Open Edit Scene and choose the app again from its current location. A moved or removed app may need to be selected again.

An outside app stayed open. Check that the scene uses Hide or Quit. Some apps may refuse a close or hide request; SceneKeeper does not force-terminate protected system apps.

Can I undo Quit? Quit may discard unsaved work if the other app has no save prompt. Review the confirmation carefully. SceneKeeper asks again every time.

Where are scenes saved? Scene definitions and interrupted new-scene drafts are stored locally on this Mac. Optional diagnostics export contains counts and filter state, not scene names or app paths.
