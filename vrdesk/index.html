Three steps

From download to workspace

Install the Mac companion

Move vrdesk to Applications, open it, then grant Screen Recording and Accessibility when macOS asks.

Open vrdesk on Quest

Keep the Mac and headset on the same trusted local network. Ethernet for the Mac gives the most reliable video.

Pair once

Enter the six-digit code shown by the Mac. Create and arrange your screens with Touch controllers.
