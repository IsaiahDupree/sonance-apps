Budget by payday.

See which bills this paycheck needs to cover and what remains safe to spend before the next one arrives.

Using PayWindow

Set a weekly, biweekly, twice-monthly, monthly, or manual pay window. Add the bills and flexible spending that belong to it. PayWindow subtracts bills due in the window and spending already recorded, then shows the remaining amount and a daily guide for the days left.

PayWindow is a budgeting calculator. It does not connect to banks or provide financial advice. Your entries stay on your device unless you choose to export a backup or diagnostic report.
