Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

PayWindow is a manual budgeting app. This policy describes information handled by the iOS app.

Information stored on your device

PayWindow stores the income amounts, pay dates, bills, spending entries, preferences, draft entries, and locally recorded feature-use counts you enter or create. These are kept in the app’s local storage on your device. PayWindow does not send this information to the developer or a server.

Exports you choose

If you choose to export a budget backup, the file contains your budgeting records, including amounts, dates, and bill or spending descriptions. If you choose to export diagnostics, the report contains app-state counts, selected filters and preferences, and locally recorded feature-use counts; it does not include the financial amounts or descriptions in your budget. iOS presents the system save or share flow, and you decide where an exported file goes.

Data collection and tracking

PayWindow has no account, bank connection, advertising, analytics service, or tracking service. The app does not collect data from the device for transmission to the developer or third parties. Apple may process information under its own services and policies when you download the app or use iOS features.

Children and financial advice

PayWindow is a general-purpose budgeting calculator and does not provide financial, investment, tax, credit, or debt advice. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
