Updated 24 October 2026: Erase this device's data removes the local routines, rides, notes, active ride and draft and pauses paired synchronization. Clear the other device separately before reconnecting; resuming sync may restore its saved library. Files you exported and workouts already saved in Apple Health remain under your control in their respective locations. Pausing sync cancels outgoing queued transfers on this device, but cannot recall a transfer already delivered to its peer or an exported backup.

ArenaCue stores your routine names, optional horse names, exercises, drafts, ride timing/events and notes in its local Application Support directory. Routines, finished rides and notes transfer to your paired device using Apple WatchConnectivity. No account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK or developer-operated cloud endpoint is used.

Apple device backups may include the local data according to your system settings. You can export a JSON library backup from the phone app. That file contains your user-entered text and rides; save/share it only where you intend. Export excludes active rides and drafts. Archiving a routine preserves its old revisions and ride history; archiving is not erasure. Use Sync & storage to erase this device’s local records and pause its paired synchronization. Clear the other device separately before reconnecting; erase exported backups wherever you saved them. Health workouts saved separately are managed in Apple's Health app.

On Watch you can explicitly choose to record a real equestrian workout in Apple Health. ArenaCue requests permission to write workouts. It does not request access to read your health history, display health measurements, collect location, or upload health data. Apple Health may collect workout sensor data while the session runs. The foreground timer works without workout authorization and pauses when backgrounded.

Public support and privacy pages are deployed. This document describes the current source implementation. The owner selected paired-simulator acceptance; physical haptics, battery and wrist-down reliability remain unverified. Signing, distribution and remaining App Review requirements are still in progress.
