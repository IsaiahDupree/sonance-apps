ArenaCue

Your riding routine, on your wrist.

Engineering build. Not currently available on the App Store. Simulator testing and release preparation are in progress.

Create your own exercises and timing, pause or skip as the ride changes, and keep a post-ride record. ArenaCue is an engineering build for Apple Watch with an iPhone and iPad companion.

Getting started

  1. Create a routine with named exercises and durations. Add your own cue and horse name if useful.
  2. Open the routine and choose Start ride. Pause, resume, skip or end when appropriate.
  3. Save the finished ride, then add notes in Ride history.
  4. Use Sync & storage for paired transfer status, JSON library backups and device erasure.

Questions and troubleshooting

Do I need an account?

No. Routines, drafts, rides and notes are stored on your device. There is no ArenaCue cloud account.

What does paired sync mean?

Apple WatchConnectivity queues routines, finished rides and notes between paired devices. Queued or delivered does not prove that the other app applied a library. Concurrent routine versions remain visible.

Can it cue me with my wrist down?

The Watch build offers an explicitly started equestrian Apple Health workout for wrist-down execution. Foreground timers pause when you leave the app. Physical cue and battery validation is still pending; no reliability guarantee is made.

How do I erase my data?

Sync & storage → Erase this device’s data removes local routines, rides, notes, the active ride and draft, then pauses paired sync. Clear both devices before reconnecting. Exported backups and workouts already saved in Apple Health remain separately managed.

Does it replace a trainer?

No. You enter the routine; ArenaCue does not assess your horse, provide safety tracking or replace coaching.
