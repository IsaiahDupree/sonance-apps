DisciplineForge Privacy

Updated October 3, 2026

Device-local learning

DisciplineForge does not send your case answers, reflections or Forge discoveries to the developer. It has no developer account, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.

Saved notebook

Case progress, up to 30 recent answers per case, your saved reflection, Forge discoveries and sound preferences are stored locally in the app's preferences. Device backups may include this local data under your Apple backup settings.

Exports and source links

You choose whether to export a case report through the system file picker. The report includes answer history, dates, explanations, your saved reflection and reference links. Review the report before sharing it. The app does not upload reports to a developer service. Opening a primary-source link uses your browser; the visited institution's privacy policy then applies.

Your controls

Guide provides separate confirmed resets for study-notebook data and Forge discoveries. If saved notebook data cannot be decoded, the app preserves it and allows an export before you choose to reset.
