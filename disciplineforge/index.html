Research Cases

The Cases tab opens four original learning exercises based on NASA, NHGRI/NCBI, USGS and NIST publications. Explore terrain-relative navigation, genome-reference organization, groundwater tracers and digital signatures. These are simplified educational interpretations, not simulations of the published systems.

  1. Choose a case and read its question and connection map.
  2. Choose a method. Read the explanation for your answer. Reconsider an alternative or continue after a supported choice.
  3. Complete the three decisions, write your reflection and choose Save reflection.
  4. Choose Review & export case report. Review your own text and select Export to save a plain-text report through the system file picker.

What is saved?

Each case keeps its current round, completion progress, latest 30 answers and a reflection of up to 4,000 characters. Your report includes recorded answers, dates, explanations, the saved reflection and primary-source links. This notebook remains on your device until you deliberately export it. It is not a proficiency certificate.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Case text and Forge exploration work offline. Source links open your browser and need an internet connection. There is no developer account, subscription, advertising or analytics service.

The Forge and Atlas

The Forge lets you select two unlocked fields and explore different synthesis lenses. Each discovery includes a relationship, an application and a reference. The Atlas organizes the fields and shows discovery progress. These discoveries are separate from case answers.

Practice and recovery

After completing a case, choose Practice another round. Your saved reflection and latest answers remain available. Guide offers separate controls for resetting the study notebook and Forge progress, with confirmation. Export reports first if you want to keep them.

If saved notebook data cannot be read, the app preserves it and pauses changes. The Cases tab offers an export of the saved data before an explicit reset.
