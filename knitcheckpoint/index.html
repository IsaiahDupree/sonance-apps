KnitCheckpoint

Find your place after an interruption.

Engineering build. Not currently available on the App Store. Physical Watch validation and release preparation are in progress.

Keep row counts, checkpoints and correction history for your own knitting projects. KnitCheckpoint is an engineering build for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Getting started

  1. Create an empty project and add the counters you need.
  2. Count rows and save a checkpoint at a known-good place.
  3. Review history when you need to correct a count or restore a checkpoint.
  4. Use local backups and exports. Paired-device and optional cloud sync are separate settings.

Questions and troubleshooting

Do I need a cloud account?

No. Counting, checkpoint recovery and backups work without an account. Optional cloud sync uses sign-in and stores your selected history and PDFs with the developer-operated service.

Where can I view PDFs?

PDF pattern viewing is on iPhone and iPad. Watch provides the wrist-counting workflow.

What happens when devices disagree?

The event journal retains offline progress and correction history. Physical disconnected-device testing is still pending; a queued sync is not a claim that every device has reconciled.

How do I erase data?

Device-only erasure clears the local archive and saved session and pauses paired sync. Cloud-and-device erasure also removes KnitCheckpoint cloud records and PDFs and fences old cloud generations. Clear old archives on other devices before reconnecting.

What does account deletion affect?

Delete sign-in account removes the shared Auth identity after password verification and confirmation. Other apps using that same identity may lose associated data. Use KnitCheckpoint-only archive erasure to retain the identity.
