KnitCheckpoint privacy

Back to support

KnitCheckpoint stores projects, counters, notes, session durations and progress history on your device. Imported PDFs are copied into the app's storage. Projects start empty. Counting, checkpoint recovery and backups do not require an account.

If you enable paired synchronization, your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange project details and progress history through Apple's WatchConnectivity service. PDF viewing remains on iPhone and iPad.

If you choose cloud sync, your email address is used for sign-in, and your project history and PDFs are stored in the shared Supabase project operated for Isaiah Dupree's apps. HTTPS protects transport and access rules limit records and files to your signed-in account. Cloud sync is not end-to-end encrypted. Session credentials are held in Keychain. The app does not save your password.

There are no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. Diagnostics are exported only when you choose and omit names, notes, patterns, email addresses and credentials. Backups and CSV exports can include personal project information; you control where to save or share them.

Moving a project to Trash preserves its history and supports restoration. Removing a pattern from a project also preserves its bytes for historical backups. Erase this device archive removes the local archive and saved session and pauses paired sync; cloud copies remain. Erase cloud and device archives deletes KnitCheckpoint cloud records and PDFs, clears this device, and prevents an old cloud generation from being re-uploaded. Clear the old archives on other devices before reconnecting them.

Delete sign-in account permanently removes the shared Auth identity after password verification and explicit confirmation. Other apps using the same identity may lose associated data; the app explains this before deletion. Use KnitCheckpoint-only archive erasure to retain the shared identity. Copies you exported or shared elsewhere remain under your control. Provider backup-retention policies may govern recovery copies outside the live application.
