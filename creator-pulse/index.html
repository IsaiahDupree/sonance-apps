Creator Pulse

Publication receipts and measured updates.

Development preview. Simulator acceptance and store preparation are in progress. This Watch release is not currently offered for download here.

Getting started

  1. Sign in to an account with an explicitly configured workspace.
  2. Select the account-scoped workspace on iPhone and pair it with Watch.
  3. Refresh to read publication states and available measured values. Unknown collection times or values remain unknown.

What to expect

A configured workspace and source publisher are required. Creator Pulse has no social publishing or scheduling controls, revenue estimates or invented metrics. Owner-feed and paired workflow acceptance remain in progress.

Pairing and offline use

Open the app on both paired devices when checking a transfer. Waiting to send is different from a confirmed result. A disconnected device may continue to show older saved records.
