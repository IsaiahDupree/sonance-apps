Creator Pulse support

Privacy policy

Creator Pulse · development preview

Account and workspace data

Creator Pulse uses Supabase for account authentication and its workspace service. Your sign-in email and authentication information are processed by that service. The workspace stores your account identifier, selected source identifiers, sanitized dated snapshots and applicable alert acknowledgement receipts. Source logs, commands, passwords, social access tokens and job-control credentials are not included in snapshots.

Device storage and pairing

The iPhone keeps its sign-in session in device-only Keychain storage. Watch receives a separate scoped credential with a maximum one-hour lifetime, rather than the phone’s sign-in session. Selected snapshots and pending receipts are cached in the app’s protected storage. No analytics, advertising or tracking SDK is included.

Sign-out and erasure

Use Sign out and revoke paired access on iPhone to remove local protected content, request paired erasure and revoke the selected phone/Watch credentials. If offline, sign-out remains unfinished until you reconnect and retry; a disconnected Watch may retain its credential until erasure arrives or the credential expires. Erase this Watch removes its local content; revoke server access from iPhone too.

Server retention and deletion

Workspace records remain until the workspace administrator removes the scope and its associated records. Source records in Orion or Airtime remain independently managed. To request workspace or account deletion, contact support from the account email. We verify the request before removing account-linked data and never ask you to send a password or verification code. This development preview does not offer public account registration. Retention automation is part of release preparation.

Support email

The email provider processes information you choose to send to support. Please omit source logs and private content. Request removal of a support message at the contact address below.
