CastDeck for macOS

Privacy Policy

How CastDeck handles media, local devices, and preferences.

CastDeck support

Last updated: October 4, 2026. Applies to CastDeck 0.1.0.

Developer data collection

CastDeck has no app account, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or developer-operated cloud storage service. CastDeck does not upload your media, receiver list, preferences, or app activity to Isaiah Dupree.

Local device discovery

The app uses your local network to discover Google Cast, AirPlay and DLNA/UPnP receivers. It also reads available CoreAudio outputs and paired Bluetooth devices from macOS. Receiver names, addresses, capabilities and connection state are used to display devices and establish the playback connections you choose.

Media playback and mirroring

Files you select remain at their original locations. For local-file casting, CastDeck temporarily makes the selected media available through an HTTP server on your Mac so the chosen receiver can stream it. App mirroring uses macOS ScreenCaptureKit after you grant Screen Recording permission; live media is served to a compatible receiver. This sends media to the playback device you select. It is not an upload to a developer server.

When you paste a direct media URL, the Mac or receiver contacts that URL's host to play the media. That host and your receiver manufacturer may process the request under their own privacy practices. CastDeck cannot control their storage or logging. Use trusted receivers, networks, and media sources.

Preferences and diagnostics

Appearance and accent settings are stored locally in macOS app preferences. Routine quality events are written to local macOS system logs, without media URLs, filenames, or identifying device records. Diagnostic tools, if explicitly enabled, can produce additional local technical logs. CastDeck has no built-in diagnostics upload.

Permissions and control

macOS manages Local Network, Bluetooth, selected-file access and Screen Recording permissions. You can change permissions in System Settings and end a playback or mirroring session in the app. CastDeck does not edit or delete your source media. Removing the app does not delete files you selected for playback.

Apple services

Apple distributes the app and provides the macOS frameworks used by CastDeck. Apple may process App Store or operating-system information under Apple's privacy policy.

Support email

If you email support, the email address, message and attachments you voluntarily provide are used to respond to your request. Do not send private media or credentials. This email is separate from CastDeck's app operation.

Policy updates and contact
