Strength Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Summary: Strength does not require an account, does not include advertising or tracking, and does not send your workout or measurement data to the developer.

Data stored on your device

The app stores workouts, exercise history, body measurements you choose to enter, custom exercises, setup preferences, and settings locally on your iPhone. This data is not transmitted to the developer. Apple may include app data in a device backup according to your iCloud and device settings.

In-app purchases

Strength uses Apple’s StoreKit to offer and restore Strength Pro Lifetime. Apple processes the transaction and may provide the app with purchase-entitlement information needed to unlock the feature. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Data collection and tracking

The current Strength app does not collect personal data, does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Permissions

Strength does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or Apple Health records for its documented features.

Children

The app is a general-audience fitness tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If app features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before those practices take effect.
