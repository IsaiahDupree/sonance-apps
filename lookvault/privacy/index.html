LookVault for iPhone, iPad & Mac

Privacy Policy

LookVault is built to work on your device. This page explains exactly what it does and does not do with your data.

Last updated: July 28, 2026 · Applies to LookVault for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

The short version

LookVault stores your closet, looks, trips, and wardrobe analytics on your device. You do not create an account to use it, and your wardrobe never leaves your device unless you actively use an optional AI feature. We do not sell your data, we do not run third-party advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles about you.

What LookVault stores on your device

Everything you add to LookVault — garments and their details, saved looks, Vault collections, trips and packing lists, cost-per-wear analytics, and your preferences — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. In Demo Mode the app runs entirely offline with sample data and no sign-up.

AI features (Ask Stylist and AI outfit suggestions)

LookVault includes optional AI features, such as Ask Stylist and AI outfit suggestions. These are used only when you choose to. When, and only when, you invoke an AI feature, the text of your request and the wardrobe details needed to answer it are sent through our secure backend to a third-party AI provider so a response can be generated and returned to you. This information is used solely to produce your result; it is not used to advertise to you and is not sold. If you never use an AI feature, none of your wardrobe data is transmitted off your device.

Purchases and subscriptions

The LookVault lifetime unlock and the LookVault AI subscription are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment; we never see or store your payment card details. Your purchase and subscription entitlements are provided to the app by Apple's StoreKit and can be restored at any time through "Restore Purchases." You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account subscription settings.

Third parties

The only third parties involved are Apple (App Store purchases, on-device storage) and the AI provider that processes AI-feature requests you initiate, as described above. LookVault does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not share your wardrobe data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

LookVault is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
