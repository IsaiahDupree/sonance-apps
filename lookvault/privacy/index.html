LookVault for iPhone, iPad & Mac

Privacy Policy

LookVault is built to work on your device. This page explains exactly what it does and does not do with your data.

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Applies to LookVault for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

The short version

Your closet content stays in your private app storage and, when enabled, your Apple iCloud container. LookVault sends only limited, content-free product analytics under a random installation identifier. It never sends your photos, garment or outfit names, trip destinations, notes, prices, or other wardrobe content. We do not sell data, run third-party advertising, or track you across apps and websites.

What LookVault stores on your device

Garment photos and details, closets, saved outfits, trips, packing lists, backups, and preferences are stored in the app's private storage. If iCloud is available for LookVault, Apple may sync eligible app data through your private iCloud container. You do not create a LookVault account, and we cannot browse the content stored in your app or iCloud account.

Anonymous product analytics

LookVault sends limited events to our Supabase analytics service so we can understand whether workflows succeed and improve reliability. These events may include a random installation identifier, random session identifier, event time, app version and build, platform, content-free actions such as completing a garment save or outfit score, foreground-active time in broad screen areas, and Apple purchase outcomes with the App Store product identifier.

Analytics do not include photos or images, garment or outfit names, wardrobe contents, trip names or destinations, location, prices, notes, filenames, file paths, free-form text, account identity, advertising identifiers, or payment-card information. Counts and timing are reduced or bucketed where practical. Background time is excluded from screen activity measurements. The app can submit analytics but cannot read analytics records or reporting views.

We use these events to measure workflow completion, time to first value, retained product value, reliability, and purchase conversion. We do not use them for advertising or cross-app tracking.

On-device analysis

LookVault can use Apple's on-device image analysis to isolate a garment and suggest a clothing category. You review those suggestions before saving. Outfit scoring uses the attributes you confirm and deterministic weather and occasion rules. AI stylist chat, AI outfit generation, and virtual try-on are not available in this release, and LookVault does not send wardrobe content to an AI provider.

Purchases and subscriptions

The optional LookVault lifetime unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment; we never see or store your payment-card details. StoreKit provides purchase status to the app and lets you restore the unlock. This release does not sell a subscription or trial.

Third parties

Apple provides app distribution, StoreKit purchases, on-device frameworks, and optional iCloud storage. Supabase receives the limited anonymous analytics described above. LookVault does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not share wardrobe content with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention and deletion

You can delete wardrobe content inside the app, replace it by restoring a backup, or remove the app and its local data through your device settings. You control data stored in iCloud through your Apple account. We retain anonymous analytics only as long as reasonably needed to measure product performance and reliability. Because analytics use a random identifier and are not linked to an account or contact information, we generally cannot identify an individual's analytics records in response to a deletion request.

Children

LookVault is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
