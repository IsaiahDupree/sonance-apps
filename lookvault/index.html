About

LookVault is a private closet and outfit planner for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Add garments from your camera or photo library, review on-device category and color suggestions, organize multiple closets, assemble and save outfits, record when you wear them, and build packing plans for trips.

Outfit Score checks your confirmed garment attributes against the occasion and weather. It is a deterministic planning tool, not an AI opinion. Your garment photos, names, outfit details, and trip details are never included in product analytics.

Getting started

  1. Create your first closet.
  2. Add a garment and confirm its category, color, material, and warmth.
  3. Choose Rate an Outfit, select pieces, and set the weather and occasion.
  4. Save outfits you like, mark them worn, or create a trip packing plan.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. LookVault has no account or sign-up. Your closet content stays in the app's private storage and your Apple iCloud container when available.

Does LookVault send my photos to AI?

No. Garment isolation and category suggestions use Apple's on-device frameworks. AI stylist chat, AI outfit generation, and virtual try-on are not available in this release.

How does Outfit Score work?

It evaluates the garment attributes you confirm against the weather and occasion. It does not upload your wardrobe or use generative AI.

Is it free?

You can create a closet with up to 30 garments and plan one trip for free. Outfit scoring and packing guidance remain free. A one-time lifetime unlock removes closet and trip limits; there is no subscription or trial in this release.

How do backups work?

Use Settings to export or restore a LookVault backup. Restoring replaces the current app data, so keep the backup file somewhere you control.
