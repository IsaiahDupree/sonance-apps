ViewHarbor

YouTube research workspace

Release status

ViewHarbor is in development. Its App Store release is being prepared. A working research-service connection is required for online research; public service access has not yet been announced.

What ViewHarbor does

ViewHarbor organizes YouTube research around topics and audience questions. Its current development version lets you save topics and source evidence on your device, inspect recorded view measurements when the connected service has them, and open the original YouTube source.

Observation dates and measured counts remain visible. Historical measurements are not forecasts or a guarantee of current momentum. Saved research is local to each device.

Connection help

  • Live research requires a compatible, reachable Market Tape service configured in Settings. A localhost address refers to the device running the app; it does not connect an iPhone to a Mac.
  • Draft generation additionally requires a compatible Script Hub service and device pairing. Do not enter provider API keys into the app.
  • Previously saved research can be reopened without fetching it again. View history still requires a service connection.
  • The Analyzer inspects only the image or video you select, using Apple's on-device frameworks. It does not upload that media.
