BusScope Support

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

BusScope reads USB and Thunderbolt device information that macOS reports on your Mac. This policy describes information handled by the macOS app.

Information processed and stored locally

When you refresh, BusScope reads device names, bus type, topology path, reported speed, vendor, and serial number when available from the local macOS system report. The app shows these details on your Mac and stores recent device state and connection-history entries locally in its application-support folder. Search, filters, appearance, language, and local feature-use counters are stored in app preferences. BusScope does not send this hardware inventory or preference data to the developer or a server.

Optional diagnostics export

If you choose to export diagnostics, the file contains aggregate connected-device counts, USB/Thunderbolt counts, history-event count, selected filters, whether the last refresh succeeded, and local feature-use counts. It does not include device names, serial numbers, or topology paths. macOS presents a save dialog and you choose the destination. If you later share the file with support, you choose to send it.

Data collection and tracking

BusScope has no account, advertising, remote analytics service, or tracking service. The app does not collect device information for transmission to the developer or third parties. Apple may process information under its own services and policies when you download the app or use macOS features.
