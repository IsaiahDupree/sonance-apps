Understand every connection.

Inspect the USB and Thunderbolt device trees reported by macOS, compare negotiated speeds, and review a local attach/detach timeline.

Using BusScope

Choose Refresh to read the USB and Thunderbolt reports from macOS. Search, filter, or sort the returned devices, then select a device to inspect its reported bus, speed, topology, vendor, and serial number. The details depend on what the device and macOS expose.

Connection history compares successful refreshes and is kept locally on this Mac. It is not a live hardware event feed; refresh BusScope after connecting or disconnecting a device.

Optional diagnostics export contains aggregate device and history counts, selected filters, refresh success, and local feature-use counts. It omits device names, serial numbers, and topology paths. Review the file before choosing to share it.
