# Driftlight privacy policy

Updated November 13, 2026. This policy covers Driftlight for iPhone and Mac.

## Your media and settings

Driftlight stores your saved looks, preferences, and imported media on your device. When you choose a photo, Live Photo, or video, the system picker shares the item you selected. The app copies that item into its local library for editing. Removing an import removes Driftlight’s copy. Rendering and clip conversion take place on your device. Saving on iPhone uses the Photos add-only permission. On Mac, a desktop picture can remain in the app container while the system uses it, and exports are written to the location you select.

## App analytics

Driftlight sends limited product analytics to a database hosted by Supabase on our behalf. The app can send a randomly generated installation identifier, app and OS information, foreground session or feature activity, export and save outcomes, and purchase or restore information. We use these events to find failures and improve the app. We do not use them for advertising or tracking across apps. Events do not contain your wallpaper images, video or photo library, filenames, file paths, saved-look names, or the contents of anything you make. Optional on-device activity records are separate. You can manage and clear them in the app.

## Purchases and support

Apple processes the one-time Complete purchase. Driftlight uses Apple’s purchase information to unlock the product, restore ownership, and measure whether the purchase flow worked. We do not receive your card number. If you email support, we receive the information you send and use it to answer your request.
