Heartline Rush

Charm the crowd, outplay rivals, and turn every scene into your story.

How to play

  • Drag anywhere to move through each scene.
  • Hold near a character to fill their heart and bring them into your crew.
  • Collect hearts for energy, reclaim admirers from rivals, and avoid professors.
  • Complete each scene's goal before time runs out.

Accessibility and settings

Music, sound effects, haptics, difficulty, and character selection can be adjusted from Settings. Heartline Rush also respects the iPhone Reduce Motion preference.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game require an account or internet?
No. Heartline Rush is playable offline and has no account or sign-in.

Where is progress stored?
Campaign progress, settings, and aggregate playtest counters are stored locally on your iPhone.

How do I replay the tutorial?
Open Settings and choose Tutorial.
