Build a route

  1. Tap Begin route 01 or open Mission map, then select a circuit.
  2. Tap cells next to the end of the route to extend it horizontally or vertically.
  3. Pass through that circuit's numbered relays in order, then reach its receiver.
  4. Complete every circuit within the shared step budget.

Leave room for every signal

Blocked cells cannot be used. Each circuit's transmitter, receiver, and relays are reserved for that circuit. Routes cannot share cells.

Every connection between neighboring cells uses one step. The budget covers all circuits together, so a route that works on its own may leave too little room for the others.

Change your approach

Tap an earlier cell on the selected route to backtrack. Undo removes one step, Clear circuit clears the selected route, and Reset mission clears all routes in the current puzzle.

There is no timer. Take the time you need to explore a different path.

Progress and stars

Complete a mission to unlock the next. Meet the three-star step target for three stars, or finish within two steps above that target for two stars, or within the mission budget for one star.

Your best stars for completed missions are saved on your device and determine which missions are unlocked. Unfinished routes are not restored after you close and reopen the app. No account or player-name form is required to play.
