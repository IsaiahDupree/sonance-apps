Heartline Rush support

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 20, 2026.

Heartline Rush does not collect or transmit personal data. It has no account, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, or developer-operated network service.

Data stored on your device

The game stores settings, campaign progress, best scores, tutorial status, and aggregate playtest counters locally using Apple's on-device storage. The aggregate counters may include completed sessions, completed tutorial steps, level outcomes, and time to a first successful charm. They do not include names, contact information, device identifiers, precise location, or gameplay recordings.

Optional copy action

If you tap Copy Data in Settings, Heartline Rush places a plain-text summary of those local aggregate counters on the system clipboard. The app does not transmit that summary. You control whether and where it is pasted or shared.

Audio, video, and permissions

The app's story scenes, music, and sound effects are bundled with the game. Heartline Rush does not access your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Children

Heartline Rush does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted here with a revised date. For privacy questions, email isaiahdupree33@gmail.com.
