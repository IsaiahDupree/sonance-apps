Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026. This policy describes the Signal Routes puzzle experience.

Playing the game

No account is required. The puzzles work offline, and you do not need to provide a name, email address, or theme prompt to play. The Signal Routes app does not send gameplay or personal information to the developer or a third-party service. It includes no analytics, advertising, tracking, or in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

Heartline Rush saves your best star count for each completed mission in local app storage. These records determine which missions are unlocked when you reopen the game. Unfinished routes are not saved across app launches. The replacement puzzle does not request or record a player name, typed theme phrase, relationship selections, or the previous game's playtest counters.

If you installed an earlier arcade version, local settings and progress from that version may remain on your device. Those older values can include a player name, theme phrase, character and relationship selections, scores, tutorial state, and aggregate playtest counters. This policy does not imply that updating the app deletes those existing local values.

Contacting support

If you choose to email support, your email provider sends the information you include, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. This is separate from playing the puzzle. Please avoid including information that is not needed to explain your issue.

This website

The support and privacy pages are static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them involves requests to the hosting provider. Its handling of those requests is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact
