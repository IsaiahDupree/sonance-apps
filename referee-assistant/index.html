Match assistant

Keep the match record on your wrist.

Development preview. Simulator acceptance and store preparation are in progress. This Watch release is not currently offered for download here.

Getting started

  1. Enter the team names and period lengths in seconds.
  2. Start or pause the clock, record scores and add event notes.
  3. Correct a score or undo a note in the event history. Export the match record when finished.

What to expect

You choose the match rules and period lengths. Optional notifications depend on system permission and delivery; keep an appropriate backup timekeeping method.

Pairing and offline use

Open the app on both paired devices when checking a transfer. Waiting to send is different from a confirmed result. A disconnected device may continue to show older saved records.
