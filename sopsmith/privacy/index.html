SOPsmith Support

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

This policy describes SOPsmith for macOS. SOPsmith processes captures and procedure content locally on your Mac.

Screen captures and project files

SOPsmith lists visible window names when you refresh the window picker. It captures only the selected window and only after you press Capture Step. Captured images, OCR title suggestions, project text, tags, and editable redaction regions are stored in local project folders in your Application Support directory. Project folders retain the original captures so you can edit your guide later.

When you export a Markdown, HTML, or PDF guide, redaction regions are rendered into the exported images. The export does not include the original unredacted capture. Review exports and project packages before sharing them, because project packages contain original screenshots and editable project text.

Diagnostics

Optional diagnostics contain aggregate document and feature-use counts and sanitized local save errors. They do not include project titles, screenshot contents, or file paths. You choose whether to export or share diagnostics.

Data collection and tracking

SOPsmith has no account, advertising, remote analytics service, or tracking service. The app does not transmit captures, project data, diagnostics, or preferences to the developer or a third-party server. On-device Vision text recognition does not send images to a remote service. Apple may process information under its own services and policies when you download the app or use macOS features.
