Make clear, editable work instructions.

Capture a selected app window when you choose, add the steps and context, cover sensitive details in exports, and keep your project files on your Mac.

Using SOPsmith

Create a project, choose a visible window, and press Capture Step when that window is ready. On-device text recognition can suggest a step title. Add instructions, audience, prerequisites, and tags, then edit, reorder, or remove steps before exporting a Markdown, HTML, or PDF guide.

Screen Recording permission is required for window capture. SOPsmith does not record automatically. Review each capture before sharing it. Redaction regions are applied to images included in exported guides; your editable project keeps the original captures and redaction settings.

Project packages and screenshots are stored locally. Optional diagnostics contain feature and document counts only, not project titles, screenshots, or paths.
