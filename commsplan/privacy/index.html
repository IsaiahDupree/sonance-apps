CommsPlan Support

Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy describes information handled by the CommsPlan iPhone app. CommsPlan does not require an account and the developer does not operate a CommsPlan account or analytics server.

Mission records stay on your device

Operation requirements, equipment profiles, sites, teams, assignments, routes, coverage areas, deployment tasks, cached terrain samples, and field checks are stored in the app's local database. CommsPlan has no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. The developer does not receive these records through an app backend.

Location and terrain

CommsPlan requests device location only when you choose Use Current Location while recording a site or field check. You can enter coordinates manually instead.

If you explicitly confirm Download Terrain Profile, CommsPlan sends 25 sampled path coordinates to the U.S. Geological Survey Elevation Point Query Service. It does not send your operation name, team, callsign, equipment inventory, or field observations in that request. Returned elevation samples and source details are cached with the operation for later offline review. The USGS receives the network request and coordinates needed to answer it.

Apple MapKit supplies map imagery and related map services. Apple may process requests under its own services and privacy terms; map availability depends on network access and cached data. CommsPlan does not promise downloadable offline basemaps.

Exports and deletion

Mission PDFs, CSV files, GeoJSON, and versioned archives are created locally. Nothing is sent to a recipient until you choose a destination through Apple's share sheet. Exported files may contain sensitive locations, frequencies, callsigns, assignments, notes, and observed coverage. Review the contents and recipient before sharing.

Deleting an operation in the app removes its locally owned records after confirmation. Deleting CommsPlan removes its private app container. Copies you previously exported or shared remain wherever you or the recipient saved them.
