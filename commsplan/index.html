CommsPlan · iPhone

Plan it. Verify it. Leave with evidence.

Build a field communications plan for a radio or volunteer team, estimate where a path may be weak, then record what operators actually observe.

What CommsPlan does

  • Keep operation requirements, teams, equipment profiles, sites, assignments, and check-in plans together.
  • Draw routes and coverage areas, review link-budget and terrain-clearance estimates, and compare candidate relay sites.
  • Prepare a PACE plan with an explicit authority-confirmation step, then use a deployment checklist in the field.
  • Record GPS-based or manually located radio checks and compare operator-confirmed observations with the original estimate.
  • Export a mission package, ICS 205-compatible CSV, field-check CSV, GeoJSON, or a versioned archive for handoff.

Core records, calculations, and exports work locally without an account. Optional terrain profiles require a network connection when downloaded and can then be reviewed offline. Apple map imagery depends on Apple's services and cached availability.

Limits and responsible use

Coverage and propagation results are screening estimates. They do not guarantee communications availability, regulatory authorization, or life-safety performance. Operators remain responsible for licensing, coordination, local rules, RF exposure, equipment safety, and field verification. CommsPlan does not automatically select legal frequencies or program radios.
