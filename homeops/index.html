Getting started

Create a home, add a component such as an appliance or system, then record verified details, maintenance, observations, work, and supporting evidence in its Component Passport.

Use a guided check to record what you observed and the next action. If a safety stop appears, pause the procedure and contact a qualified professional when appropriate. HomeOps does not diagnose emergencies or replace professional inspection.

Photos, reports, and backups

Camera or photo access is requested only when you choose to capture or import an image. Records and selected photo copies are stored in the app's local device storage. Exported reports and JSON records are shared only when you choose a destination.

Exports can contain addresses, equipment details, notes, costs, and photos. Check the contents and destination before sharing. Device backup behavior follows your Apple account and device settings.
