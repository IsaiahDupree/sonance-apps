HomeOps Support

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

This policy describes the HomeOps iPhone app. HomeOps does not require an account. The developer does not operate a HomeOps analytics, advertising, cloud-sync, or remote-configuration service.

Home records stay on your device

Home names, component details, maintenance, observations, work history, costs, notes, generated reports, and selected evidence are stored in the app's local database and app-owned device storage. HomeOps does not send these records to a developer-operated server.

Photos and camera

Photo or camera permission is requested only after you choose an image capture or import action. Selected photos are copied into HomeOps app-owned storage and associated with your local record. The app does not upload those photos to the developer.

Sharing and deletion

Reports and structured exports are generated locally. HomeOps sends an export to another app, person, or location only when you choose a destination in Apple's share interface. An export may include home addresses, equipment details, costs, notes, and photos; review it before sharing.

Use the in-app erase action to remove HomeOps records and generated-report caches from the app. Copies already shared elsewhere remain under the control of the destination. Device backups follow your Apple account and device settings.

Security limits

HomeOps relies on the iOS app sandbox and operating-system device protections. The prototype does not add its own encryption key or account recovery. Do not use it as a production vault for highly sensitive identity or insurance documents.
