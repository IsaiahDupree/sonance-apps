How to play

Steer the car with the on-screen pad and use BRAKE when a turn needs it. Find all ten signals to finish. The daily island and your attempts are available on your device; if the online service is unavailable, the game reports that your result stayed local.

When online results are enabled and reachable, you can compare a daily score and see replay ghosts. Online names and gameplay replays may be visible to other players.

Questions and support

The leaderboard did not load

Check your connection and try again later. Your locally saved run remains available on the device. A leaderboard appears only when the game build is configured for the service and that service is reachable.

How do I request deletion of online data?

Signal Hunt does not currently offer an in-game server-data deletion control. Contact support with the app name, your display name, and the approximate UTC day you played. Do not include passwords, session tokens, or other account credentials.
