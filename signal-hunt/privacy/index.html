Signal Hunt Support

Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy describes the Signal Hunt iOS game and its optional online leaderboard and replay features. Signal Hunt does not require an email address, phone number, or real-name account.

Offline play and device storage

The daily island, player settings, display name, local attempts, personal-best data, and replay frames are stored on your device. Replay frames describe the generated in-game course and include gameplay timing, position and orientation, speed, vehicle state, signal-collection events, and camera events. The game does not use your real-world location for the island or replay. Removing the app or its data removes these local records.

Optional online features

When an installed build is configured for the online service and you use an online feature, the game creates or refreshes an anonymous Supabase Auth session. The service receives an installation identifier, your chosen or generated display name, a player identifier, the daily challenge identifier, run time and score, completion and collection results, and replay frames. These records support the leaderboard and online replay ghosts. Display names, scores, and associated game replays may be shown to other players.

The app does not send email, phone, contact-list, advertising, or real-world location data through this game service. If the service is not configured or cannot be reached, the core game continues locally and the app reports that the result was not synced. Supabase provides the hosted authentication and database service for online features and processes requests under its own terms and privacy policy.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

The current project configuration contains no advertising or analytics SDK. If you choose to save or share a replay video using the iOS system interface, the destination you select receives the content under that service's own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Online profile and run records do not have a published automatic expiry period. Uninstalling Signal Hunt removes local app data but does not delete records already sent to the online service. The game does not currently include an in-app control to delete online records. To request deletion, contact support with the app name, your display name, and the approximate UTC day of a run so the correct anonymous profile can be located. Do not send a password or session token.

Children and contact

Signal Hunt does not ask for a child's name or contact details. Parents or guardians can contact the developer with privacy questions or a deletion request at isaiahdupree33@gmail.com.
