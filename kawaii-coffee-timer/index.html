About Kawaii Coffee Timer

Kawaii Coffee Timer combines a cozy Pomodoro-style focus timer with guided timers for pour-over, French press, AeroPress, espresso, and cold brew. You can adjust coffee and water amounts, save a brew history, organize beans, review approximate caffeine totals, earn achievements, and unlock visual customizations with points earned in the app.

An account is optional. Choose Continue without account to use the core timers and keep data on the device. Email-based accounts use Supabase for authentication and selected profile or onboarding information. The current App Store release has no live subscription catalog; free timer features remain available.

Getting started

  1. Complete the short setup, then choose Continue without account or create an optional email-based account.
  2. Use Timer for a customizable focus-and-break cycle, or open Brew and select a guided coffee method.
  3. For a guided brew, set the coffee amount, follow each timed pour or wait step, and let the app record the completed session locally.
  4. Use History, Beans, and Awards to review sessions, organize coffee, and track progress.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The core timers work without an account. Choose Continue without account during setup or sign-in to use guest mode with local storage.

Why did the app ask for notification permission?

After your first completed guided brew, the app may ask permission to schedule local timer and reminder notifications. Those alerts are created on your device. You can turn notification access off at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

Why are subscriptions unavailable?

The current App Store release does not contain a live purchase configuration or purchasable subscription catalog. The app keeps the free coffee-timer experience available and does not charge you from the unavailable screen.

How do I erase information on my device?

Open Settings and choose Reset All Data to clear timer progress and customizations. You can also delete the app to remove its local app container, subject to Apple’s backup behavior.

How do I request deletion of an account?

Email isaiahdupree33@gmail.com from the address associated with the account and use the subject Kawaii Coffee Timer account deletion. Support may verify ownership before deleting hosted account data.

Are caffeine totals medical advice?

No. Caffeine totals are rough estimates based on brew records and a general coffee-dose assumption. They are not a measurement, diagnosis, or medical recommendation.

What information leaves my device?

Guest timer and journal use is local. If you create an account, authentication and selected account or onboarding information are processed by Supabase. The current release does not use advertising, cross-app tracking, a live purchase service, or configured production analytics. See the app-specific Privacy Policy for the complete data flow.
