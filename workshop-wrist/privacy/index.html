Workshop Wrist support

Privacy policy

Workshop Wrist · development preview

Data and storage

Workshop Wrist stores the records you enter on your device and transfers them to its paired companion when you request synchronization. It does not upload those records to a developer-operated server and contains no advertising or analytics SDK. Device backups may be managed separately through Apple and your device settings.

Exports and support

Exports contain the selected records and history. You choose where to share them; recipients keep their own copies. If you email support, the email provider processes the information you send. Please omit private records, passwords and verification codes. Request removal of a support message by contacting the same address.

Erasure and paired copies

Erasing a scope removes its local content and history while retaining a marker that prevents old transfers from restoring erased content. A disconnected companion applies erasure after it receives that marker. Existing exports and device backups remain separately managed. Delete or erase the other device’s copy too when you cannot reconnect it.
