Getting started

  1. Create an ordered checklist and work notes on iPhone.
  2. Check off steps on your paired Watch. Corrections remain in revision history.
  3. Use Sync with companion to retry delivery. Export records before removing a project.

What to expect

Your instructions guide the work. Workshop Wrist does not control equipment or certify a procedure.

Pairing and offline use

Open the app on both paired devices when checking a transfer. Waiting to send is different from a confirmed result. A disconnected device may continue to show older saved records.
